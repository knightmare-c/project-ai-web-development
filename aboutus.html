About IronPulse

Building Stronger Bodies & Stronger Community

We are a homegrown Singapore fitness studio on a mission to make serious training welcoming, effective and sustainable for everyone.

Our Story

IronPulse Fitness Studio was founded in 2015 by two former athletes, Ken Wong and Sara Lim, who believed that world-class fitness coaching should be personal, approachable and results-driven.

What began as a single 200-square-metre training space near Orchard Road has grown into one of Singapore's most trusted boutique gyms — serving hundreds of members, from first-time exercisers to competitive athletes.

Today, our certified coaches, modern equipment and tight-knit community help thousands of people each year move better, feel stronger and build lasting healthy habits.
